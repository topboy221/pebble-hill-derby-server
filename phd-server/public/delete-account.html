Delete your account

Soapcube Racing

You can delete your Soapcube Racing account here, or in the game under Account → Delete account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, password and racer name.
  • Your progress: best times, career progress, cars you own and unlocks.
  • Your cash and gold balances, and the record of how they changed.

This happens straight away and can't be undone. Gold you bought is lost with the account and isn't refunded.

What we keep

  • Records of real-money purchases (gold packs), for as long as Italian tax and accounting law requires: usually up to 10 years. They aren't used for anything else.

Can't sign in?

If you forgot your password, reset it in the game (Account → Sign in → Forgot password) and come back here. Or email privacy@soapcuberacing.world from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We'll delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

Played only as a guest? Then there's no account to delete: your progress is only on your device, and goes away when you delete the app or clear its data.
